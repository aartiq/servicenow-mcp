# Governance and continuity

NowAIKit is built and maintained by **AARTIQ Ltd** (United Kingdom). It is a single-vendor open project: one accountable company owns the roadmap, review, and releases, and the source is open for anyone to read, run, fork, and contribute to.

## Maintainer

- AARTIQ Ltd, lead maintainer. Contact: enterprise@nowaikit.com

We run a focused-vendor model on purpose. One team is accountable for quality and support, rather than diffuse ownership. Contributions from the community are welcome and reviewed on top of that.

## Release cadence

NowAIKit ships often, usually several releases a month. See [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) and the npm version history for the record. Frequent, dated releases are the clearest evidence the project is active and maintained.

## Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Issues suitable for newcomers are labelled `good first issue`. External pull requests are reviewed and merged by the maintainer.

## Continuity: what happens if AARTIQ ever stepped away

You are not exposed to a single point of failure:

- **Source available** under the Elastic License 2.0. You can read, run, and fork the code.
- **Perpetual self-host rights.** Your deployed copy keeps working. There is no phone-home and no kill switch.
- **Perpetual fork rights.** If maintenance ever stopped, you or the community could fork and continue, with no permission needed.
- **Commercial support and SLA** available from AARTIQ for production use.

For most enterprises this is a stronger guarantee than a purely community-run project: an accountable vendor with a support contract, plus the freedom to carry on yourself if you ever had to. Enterprise buyers evaluating continuity should contact enterprise@nowaikit.com.
